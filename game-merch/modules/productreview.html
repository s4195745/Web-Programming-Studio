★★★★★ 5.0 out of 5

I bought this to clip onto my backpack for university and it has genuinely held up better than I expected. The plush material is soft but the seams are tight, so there's no fraying even after being tossed around in my bag every day for two weeks. The colours match the product photos exactly, and the clip attachment feels sturdy. Would definitely buy other characters from this line.
