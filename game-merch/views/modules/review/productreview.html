Cute set, sticks well to the fridge

Your review

Product: Hollow Knight Fridge Magnet – The Knight & Hornet · Reviewed by Hoang Xuan Quynh · 28 Jun 2026

★★★★★ 5.0 out of 5

I bought this magnet set as a small gift to myself after finishing the game and it's held up on my fridge for weeks now without sliding off or fading in the sunlight from the kitchen window. Both the Knight and Hornet designs are printed sharp with no blurring around the linework, and the magnet strength is strong enough to pin a couple of postcards underneath as well. Would happily buy the rest of the set.
